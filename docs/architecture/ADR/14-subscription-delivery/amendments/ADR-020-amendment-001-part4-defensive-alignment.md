# ADR-020 Amendment 001 — Part 4 Defensive Alignment

**Parent:** ADR-020 — Subscription & Multimodal Delivery Optimisation  
**Status:** Applied / Reconciled  
**Date:** 2026-08-22

## Decision

ADR-020 remains authoritative for the subscription and multimodal delivery decision. It inherits Part 4 controls relevant to API security, external partners, economic abuse, trust, monitoring, resilience, scalability and assurance.

## Customer Authority

Security and optimisation controls must not silently convert subscription or delivery automation into unrestricted autonomous authority. Customer intent, authorisation, policy and applicable human-approval requirements remain authoritative.

## Provider Neutrality

External transport and payment providers remain replaceable through governed integration boundaries.

## Closure

Resolves GAP-009. Existing ADR-020 forward-consistency and closure records remain valid.
